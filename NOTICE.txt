The original bridge incorporates Tencent/openclaw-weixin protocol and media
work and silk-wasm support. Their MIT notices are preserved under licenses/.
Tencent reference revision: 24de5c9eb0dd5e595d7e2d090ed8a3f82870d42c;
the Markdown filter is copied from that reference. The optional audio helper
uses silk-wasm 3.7.1. Runtime dependencies are installed separately with their
distribution notices; FFmpeg is optional and is not bundled in this repository.
OpenAI tunnel-client is a separate user-supplied official binary and is not
bundled here. wechat2dot is an independent community project, not an official
OpenAI or Tencent product.
