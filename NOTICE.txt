The wechat_dot_bridge package, original tests and legacy installation/upgrade
helpers retain their upstream structure. New wechat2dot package, onboarding,
demo, documentation, packaging and CI are contributions to this derivative.
Upstream real-world validation is not validation of this derivative deployment.

The original bridge incorporates Tencent/openclaw-weixin protocol and media
work and silk-wasm support. Their MIT notices are preserved under licenses/.
The original technical audit is retained in docs/upstream/AUDIT.md as provenance.
Some links there describe upstream history rather than this repository.

OpenAI tunnel-client is a separate user-supplied official binary and is not
bundled here. wechat2dot is an independent community project, not an official
OpenAI or Tencent product.
